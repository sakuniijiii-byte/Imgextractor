What gets exported?

    
PNG files are placed in an images/ folder. A manifest.json records the question ID, printed question number, source page, figure type, confidence and any uncertainty noted by Gemini.

    
Important: This tool renders each PDF page and uses AI bounding boxes to crop figures. It does not recover original embedded image objects. Gemini may miss small figures, merge nearby diagrams, or associate a figure with the wrong question. Always inspect the previews.
